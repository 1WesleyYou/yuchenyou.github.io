Presented at the IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)
2025 Workshop in Atlanta, GA, where it received the **Best Poster Award**.

The work combines a Kresling-origami structure with pneumatic actuation to build a
modular soft robotic arm. My contribution was the embedded real-time control stack:
STM32 and Orange Pi firmware (PID control and dynamics), CAN/I²C communication buses,
the C++/Rust control core, and part of the PCB design.
