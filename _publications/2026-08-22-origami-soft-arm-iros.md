Accepted (August 2026) as a **Late Breaking Results poster** at the 2026 IEEE/RSJ
International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), held September 27 –
October 1, 2026 in Pittsburgh, PA.

**Authors:** Mingxuan He, Jiyang Wang, Yuchen You, Jiaqi Wang, Xiaonan Huang\*

The work scales the Kresling-origami soft arm to a larger structure that sustains a
high payload. My contribution was on the embedded real-time control and firmware
stack (STM32 and Orange Pi, CAN/I²C, PID and dynamics, C++/Rust core).
